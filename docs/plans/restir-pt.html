The noise floor is the input's: the light is found by chance. ReSTIR PT (Lin et al. 2022, generalized resampled importance sampling) reuses whole paths, including ones whose first bounces are near or glossy, so the rare paths that find the patch, the window or a lamp are shared across pixels and frames instead of averaged away. The goal is RR's input closer to the fed case: brightness within a few % of the reference and drift a fraction of today's.

Decisions you are most likely to tweak

D1. What a PT sample is, and what PT covers (scope)

PT owns the indirect light at sample 0's primary vertex, exactly the integral ReSTIR GI owns today (D1 of the GI plan: eligibility at the vertex, emission and DI left where they are). ReSTIR DI keeps the direct light. A sample is the path from the primary vertex on: the random seed its prefix was drawn with, the index k of its reconnection vertex, that vertex's position and normal, the radiance the path carries back from it (everything gathered past it, SHaRC answers included), and the prefix's pdfs needed for the shift's Jacobian.

Eligibility drops GI's thresholds: every primary vertex but glass and true mirrors takes part; a path with no reconnectable vertex is still a sample (replay-only), shared through random replay.

D2. The shift: hybrid random replay + reconnection (core algorithm)

To move a sample to another pixel's vertex: replay its random numbers from the new vertex (re-tracing the prefix through the new geometry) up to vertex k, then reconnect to k's stored position. k is the first vertex that is rough (perceptual roughness >= 0.35) at both k-1 and k and far enough from k-1 (> 3 % of the camera distance: the rule ReSTIR GI's corner flecks taught). The Jacobian is the replayed prefix's pdf ratio times the reconnection's geometry term; exact pdfs are therefore required (D3). Replay that diverges (hits something else, a different lobe) makes the shift fail: zero target, counted in the MIS.

Cost: replay traces up to k-1 rays per shift (most paths reconnect at k = 1 or 2).

D3. Exact sampling pdfs first (prerequisite)

sampleDisneyBRDF picks its lobe by Fresnel at a random VNDF half vector, so the density of a sampled direction cannot be evaluated exactly (GI lived with layeredPdf's approximation). Replay needs the true pdf. Change: lobe selection by a probability that depends only on the vertex (Fresnel at n.v, or albedo/metal weights), evaluated identically in layeredPdf. Verified per material against references (ratio 1.000 to the noise floor, furnace-style uniformly lit spheres).

D4. Reuse, and RR, from day one (quality vs. denoiser)

Temporal then spatial reuse, pairwise MIS (as DI and GI). Ray Reconstruction punished correlated input (GI's spatial clumps, GI's temporal boil), so decorrelation is part of the design, not a fix later: small M caps, a spatial radius chosen by measurement, a limit on how many pixels may take the same sample in a frame (a per-sample duplication count), and every phase judged both in the reference (bias) and through RR (ratio, drift, the spheres) with the capture tool. Temporal reuse ships only if it passes the RR gate (D7).

D5. Where the passes run (frame order, cost)

The shift traces rays (replay), so the reuse passes are raygens (one SBT region each, like gi_shade: the shadow any-hit alpha test, glass pass-through and analytic spheres come for free), not compute. Order in the view lane's back half: trace (writes candidates) -> pt_temporal -> pt_spatial -> pt_shade -> accumulate -> DLSS.

Risks

  • RR and correlation: the central risk (GI's lesson). Measured every phase; if reuse cannot pass RR's gate, the fallback is PT at fewer neighbours with duplication limits, and SHaRC carrying more.
  • Replay cost: up to k-1 rays per shift; capped by the reconnection rule (most k = 1-2).
  • Exact pdfs and layered materials (coat, translucency): each lobe's selection must be reproducible on replay.
  • RR's energy loss growing with resolution (0.83 at 557x313 trace, 0.58 at 1920x1080): measured as PT lands; may need input density targets per resolution.
