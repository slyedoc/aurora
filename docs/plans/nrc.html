NRC (Müller, Rousselle, Novák, Keller 2021) learns outgoing radiance as a function of position, direction and material, online, from the frame's own paths. A path that has spread far ends in the network's answer instead of tracing on: every path end returns light, at a fixed cost per pixel, and unlike a voxel grid the answer follows the surface's direction and material. NVIDIA ships it beside SHaRC in RTXGI 2.0 for tensor-core GPUs.

Decisions you are most likely to tweak

D1. Inline or deferred inference: decided by a retest (architecture)

July's load-bearing finding: cooperative-vector matmuls faulted in the ray tracing stage (Xid 13/31/32/109, driver 580) and ran correctly in compute. Phase 0 retests on driver 615 in aurora, with a stub-vs-live A/B over thousands of frames (the July playbook: Aftermath does not dump these).

  • Inline (if the retest is clean): the raygen evaluates the network at the termination vertex, like a SHaRC query. No new passes; the answer feeds the path's sums directly, so ReSTIR PT, the meter and the reference need nothing.
  • Deferred (if it faults, the default plan): the raygen writes a query record and the throughput it would scale; a compute pass infers; a resolve pass adds radiance × throughput where the path would have (D4). More plumbing, but no dependence on a driver promise that broke once.

Either way training is compute (batched by nature). Recommendation: build deferred unless the retest is clean on two drivers' worth of evidence (this one plus a soak of 10 minutes of Northshire), since a fault takes the GPU down.

D2. Where paths end in the cache (bias vs. density vs. cost)

  • Spread termination (the paper's; July measured rel-RMSE 14.9 % against 34 % for a fixed bounce): a path ends at vertex n once its accumulated footprint a(x1..xn) = (Σ√(d2/(p cos)))2 exceeds c · a0, a0 = d02/(4π cos) at the primary vertex, c = 0.01. A path that stays sharp (mirrors, glossy) traces on.
  • Never at or before ReSTIR PT's x_k. The cache answers only past x_k for PT paths, so its light arrives through x_k's continuation (Lc, with x_k's BSDF ratio for shifted paths), and the shift's domain is unchanged. SHaRC answering at x_k needed a direction-free part and a training restriction we never explained; this avoids both. Paths outside PT (sample 0 without a PT vertex, mirrors, glass) end by the same rule from their first bounce.
  • Roulette stays after the termination test: a path the cache did not end may still roulette.

  • Buffers per camera: queries (width × height), training ring 64k records, network 6 × 64 × 64 f16 weights (~50 KB) plus Adam state. A few MB at 4K.
  • Deferred resolve destinations: the noisy colour (exposure applied, as gi_shade adds), the exposure meter's buffer, and, for a PT-live path, the candidate's Lc (radiance × throughput / gi_mask), before gi_candidates computes targets.

D5. The cache frame (large worlds)

The network learns a function of position, so positions must not move with the camera every frame (aurora's world is camera-relative). A cache frame is anchored at a world point snapped to a 64 m grid near the camera, with a 512 m box; when the camera leaves the inner half the frame recentres and the network keeps training (it re-learns in a second or so; July's training converged in ~5k steps at 234 steps/s). One network per camera; a headset's two eyes share one.

Risks

  • The RT-stage fault returns. Deferred inference is the plan of record for that reason; the retest only chooses the cheaper path.
  • Cache bias in the abbey (sparse, high-contrast light is hard to learn). The spread rule keeps early bounces traced; if bias fails the gate, raise c or train more paths before changing the network.
  • RR and a smooth cache. NRC answers are low-noise and correlated across pixels: RR may oversmooth or read them as signal (the temporal-reuse lesson). Measured by the RR gates, not assumed.
  • Training stability under motion (the cache frame recentring, fast camera). Moving captures and the sun jump cover it.
  • Slang's cooperative-vector support is newer than July's WGSL fork: training may need coopVecOuterProductAccumulate or a coopmat path; the gym decides.
