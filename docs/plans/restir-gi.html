aurora ReSTIR GI

Reservoir resampling of the first indirect bounce at sample 0's primary vertex (Ouyang et al. 2021, "ReSTIR GI"), as passes beside ReSTIR DI. Written 2026-10-15. Implementation notes: restir-gi-notes.md.

Status 2026-10-15: implemented, phases 0-4: on by default (spatial reuse; temporal off), SHaRC deleted. Measurements, decisions and deviations (gi_shade as a raygen, the 64-byte reservoir with the stored vertex, the array-free spatial pass) in restir-gi-notes.md.

Why. ReSTIR DI fixed the direct term from the light table at 1 spp; the indirect term is still one BRDF ray per pixel, with SHaRC answering past the first bounce. That one ray decides the look of every interior lit by bounce light (the abbey, sun through a doorway, a lamp around a corner), and at 1 spp Ray Reconstruction throws away its rare bright samples as fireflies, the same failure ReSTIR DI fixed for lamps. Reusing the secondary hit and its radiance across pixels and frames gives each pixel many effective indirect samples for one ray plus reuse passes.

today:   fog grid → restir grid → primary → di temporal → di spatial → trace (shades DI winner, BRDF bounce adds indirect to acc) → dlss
planned: fog grid → restir grid → primary → di temporal → di spatial → trace (writes the GI candidate, keeps its indirect out of acc)
                                                                     → gi temporal → gi spatial → gi_shade (+= dlss_color, meter) → dlss

Since ec94ef0 the view lane records in two halves: everything after the first view's primary pass is the back half (it waits on the async light grids). The GI passes sit in the back half, between the trace and DLSS, on the graphics queue.

Decisions you are most likely to tweak

D1. What GI owns, and what stays in the path (the integral split)

The radiance at the primary vertex splits into two integrals that never overlap, so nothing is counted twice and either side can be switched off alone:

GI (reservoirs, gi_shade)Path tracer, unchanged (acc)
light reflected by the secondary hit x_s toward the primary vertex x_v: everything the path gathers from x_s on (its NEE, sun, sky NEE, deeper bounces, SHaRC answers)emission at x_s and the sky on a miss (they keep their MIS weights against DI and the sky NEE at x_v)
only when x_v is eligible: perceptual roughness ≥ restir_gi_roughness and no glass transmissionevery ineligible x_v (mirrors, glossy floors, glass): their indirect stays the traced path, as today
only when x_s is a plain surface: rough (≥ the same threshold), not glass, not an emittera glossy / glass x_s (its radiance is too view-dependent to reconnect from another pixel); a fog scatter or portal on the segment
sample 0 onlysamples 1.. (accumulation modes): their indirect as today; the GI term counts as sample 0's share (/ SAMPLES), as the DI winner does

The eligibility tests are functions of the surface and the sampled direction, not of history, so the split is exact. Alpha pass-through between x_v and x_s keeps the candidate (x_s is the first accepted surface; the visibility ray sees cutouts through the any-hit shader).

D2. The candidate's weight: the whole layered sampler as one mixture (estimator)

The path picks transmission / coat / base and weights by that lobe alone. The GI candidate instead takes W = 1 / layeredPdf(v, n, ω) and the integrand evalLayered(v, n, ω, coat) · |n·ω| · L (one-sample MIS with the balance heuristic). Any lobe's direction is then a valid candidate, and reuse at another surface needs only evalLayered + layeredPdf, both of which exist. Target p̂ = luma(that integrand).

  • L is recovered as SHaRC does it: snapshot acc and mask at x_s (after its emitter check), and at the path's end L = (acc − acc_snap) / mask_snap; then acc = acc_snap.
  • Roulette: mask_snap is taken before the bounce's roulette boost (ROULETTE_FROM 1 rolls right after the primary bounce). A path roulette kills there becomes a null candidate (L = 0, M = 1), not a missing one; survivors carry the 1/q boost inside L.
  • A failed BRDF sample is a null candidate too (M still counts).

Alternative if the mixture weight proves noisy on coated stone: restrict GI to the base lobe (integrand scaled by (1−t)(1−q)) and leave coat / translucency picks to acc.

D3. Data model: candidate and reservoir (data model)

// Written by `main` per traced pixel (sample 0), read by gi_temporal as the canonical sample.
// 32 bytes: two 16-byte stores / loads.
public struct GiCandidate {
    public float3 x_s;        // secondary hit (world)
    public uint   n_s_oct;    // its geometric normal, oct 2xf16
    public uint   L;          // radiance leaving x_s toward x_v, RGB9E5 (nits)
    public float  pdf;        // layeredPdf of the direction at x_v (0 = null candidate)
    public uint   frame;      // marks it live; an ineligible pixel keeps an old one
    public uint   view_t;     // aerial-perspective x cloud transmittance at x_v (D6), RGB9E5
};

// Final / temporal reservoirs, one per traced pixel. 48 bytes: three 16-byte loads.
// x_v is not stored: rebuilt from the pixel, `depth` and that frame's inverse view-projection.
public struct GiReservoir {
    public float3 x_s;        // the winning secondary hit
    public uint   n_s_oct;
    public uint   L;          // the winner's radiance, RGB9E5
    public float  W;          // unbiased contribution weight (0 after an occluded visibility ray)
    public float  M;          // history length
    public float  phat;       // the winner's target at x_v (spatial pairwise MIS never re-evaluates it)
    public float  depth;      // view depth (clip w) at x_v: rebuilds x_v for the Jacobian
    public uint   n_v_oct;    // x_v's shading normal (temporal similarity)
    public uint   frame;
    public uint   flags;      // bit 0: the winner is this frame's own candidate (no visibility ray)
};

Per view, at trace resolution: one candidate buffer + three reservoir buffers (last frame's final, this frame's final, temporal scratch; DI's ping-pong). 176 B/px: 3840x1080 Balanced traces ~2227x626 = 245 MB per view, ~490 MB in XR. Freed when GI is off.

Shrunk up front (XR doubles everything): radiance and the atmosphere multiplier as RGB9E5, and x_v rebuilt from depth instead of stored (+ALU in the passes). Unshrunk it was 240 B/px, 670 MB in XR. Further option: the spatial pass writes over the candidate buffer (needs the canonical read first, one barrier).

D4. Reuse: what is reused and how hard (quality vs. bias)

  • Shift: reconnection at x_s (keep x_s and L, change x_v). Jacobian J = (|cos φ_new| / |cos φ_old|) · (|x_old − x_s|² / |x_new − x_s|²), φ the angle at x_s between n_s and the direction to the vertex. A shift with J outside [1/8, 8] or a back-facing x_s is rejected (a neighbour's sample from across a corner).
  • Temporal: canonical = this frame's candidate (M = 1), merged with last frame's final at the reprojected pixel (DI's reproject + similar), its x_v from the reservoir. History cap restir_gi_history, default 8 (DI's: Ray Reconstruction wants decorrelated input).
  • Spatial: DI's pairwise MIS over restir_gi_spatial_samples (default 3) neighbours in a restir_gi_spatial_radius disc (default 30 px), each tested with similar on the shared RestirSurface buffer. No neighbour visibility (biased, as DI); the final winner gets one visibility ray (D5).
  • Fireflies: no clamp on the candidate's L; the shared restir_boiling filter applies to GI's spatial pass too (one knob for both). If a lone hot L still spreads into a disc, a per-candidate luminance clamp at k x the neighbourhood mean is the next lever.
  • Moving geometry: x_s is stored in world space and not re-projected; a sample on a moving object is stale for at most the history cap. The visibility ray catches what moved in between.

D5. Where the winner is shaded: a gi_shade compute kernel with an inline ray query (frame order, cost)

The candidate exists only after main has traced, so the reuse passes run after it and a third pass shades the winner: rebuild the material from RestirSurface, evaluate evalLayered · |cos| · L · W, one visibility ray from x_v to x_s (skipped when flags bit 0 says the winner is the pixel's own fresh candidate: it was just traced), and add the result to dlss_color. An occluded winner zeroes its reservoir's W and keeps M, as DI.

It is a compute kernel in restir_gi.slang with a RayQuery (as the picking kernel), not a raygen: this is the same shape as the restir_shade pass deleted in the primary-pass work (0.32 ms), and as a raygen it would inherit the RT pipeline's 126-register allocation, an SBT region and its own ray stack. A compute kernel gets its own register count (the driver reports it, VK_KHR_pipeline_executable_properties, which it does not for RT pipelines) and writes dlss_color through a heap storage-image mapping like the fog grid's volumes.

Catch: alpha cutouts. An inline ray query runs no any-hit shader. Opacity micromaps answer baked cutouts in hardware; a hit on an unknown-state micro-triangle or an un-baked cutout needs a manual alpha test in the query loop (the triangle's UVs and alpha texture), or it counts as opaque and foliage darkens the bounce light behind it. Phase 0 decides: if the manual test is cheap enough, keep compute; otherwise fall back to a raygen with traceVisible and record the cost difference in the notes.

Async queue: no help on a flat view (DLSS waits on gi_shade and nothing runs beside it). In XR, view 0's GI passes could overlap view 1's front half; that is the cross-view overlap left for later, not part of this plan.

Rejected: shading in next frame's main (no extra pass, but a frame of lag, a second shift from last frame's vertex, and the fresh candidate missing from what is shown). Rejected for now: proposing the GI winner into main's ShadowPick (impossible: the reservoir is not ready while main runs).

D6. What gi_shade must reproduce from main's epilogue (correctness)

  • Exposure: dlss_color is pre-exposed: add gi · input_exposure, clamped to f16 like main.
  • Atmosphere (aerial perspective and the cloud shell, Sky::Atmosphere only; not a darkness fix, so GI does not replace it): atmoComposite is affine in the surface colour (col = Tc·(La + Ta·s) + …), so the GI term only needs the multiplier Ta·Tc. main computes it anyway; a split atmoCompositeT returns it and main writes it into the candidate (view_t). No second aerial / cloud march.
  • Fog veil: nothing: the composite applies guide_fog after Ray Reconstruction to the whole surface radiance.
  • Meter: pc.lum[2i] gains luma(gi) · fog_t / meter_albedo (same divisor as main, from the primary record; skipped when not metered or the meter is null, which it is for a camera's second view since c7a5861: in XR only the left eye meters).

D8. Does GI retire SHaRC? (simplification)

SHaRC went in (30be5eb) to fix darkness at 1 spp: Ray Reconstruction threw away the rare bright indirect paths as fireflies, and the abbey came out 25 % dark. ReSTIR GI fixes the same problem the unbiased way, by reusing those rare paths instead of averaging them away inside a voxel. If GI alone gets the interiors there, the cache is a second biased system doing the same job:

  • Until phase 3, SHaRC stays as is. Its answers past x_s go into L (cheaper, shorter candidate paths).
  • Phase 3 compares four images against a 4 spp reference (no cache, no reuse): GI + SHaRC, GI only, SHaRC only (today), neither. It compares error, boil and busy time, in the abbey, the lamp-lit plaza at night and the noon bench.
  • If GI alone is within a few % of GI + SHaRC, delete SHaRC in a follow-up. That removes sharc.rs, sharc.slang, the resolve on the async queue, three stack slots of snapshots in main, PATH_SHARC_TRAIN/FROM_ROUGH/CACHE_BLIND, and two settings. Deeper bounces then stay plain paths, and reuse averages their noise at x_s.
  • If the cache still pays (bounce 2+ light in deep interiors), keep both and say why in the notes.

Risks

  • Ray Reconstruction and correlated input. Reused samples are correlated across pixels and frames; RR was trained on independent 1 spp noise. DI at history 8 / 2 neighbours was fine; GI's samples are larger in footprint. Watch for blotches and smearing on camera motion; the history cap is the first lever.
  • SHaRC inside L. Cache answers are biased and blocky at their voxel size; reuse spreads them across pixels. Expected harmless (the cache only answers past the first indirect hit, when the cone covers a voxel); compare against SHaRC off in phase 2.
  • Raygen stack / registers. main gains two snapshots (acc, mask: 6 floats) and an eligibility flag live across the bounce loop. The stack is what trace time follows (352 → 256 B was worth 0.09 ms, −7 % of the trace): the candidate write must happen at the path's end from those two values only, and the logged stack is checked right after phase 0. SHaRC's three snapshot slots (D8) are the offset.
  • Memory in XR. ~490 MB for two views even shrunk (D3); with DLSS, ReSTIR DI and the guides on a 32 GB card it fits, but it is the first thing to look at if XR memory gets tight.
  • Mixture pdf vs. the real sampler. sampleDisneyBRDF picks its lobe by Fresnel at the sampled half vector; pdfDisneyBRDF at normalize(l + v). The MIS code already lives with this; phase 0's on/off match is where a mismatch would show.
  • XR. Two eyes, two views, two buffer sets, as DI; every per-view cost doubles (the budget's ~2 ms). Test after phase 2.
